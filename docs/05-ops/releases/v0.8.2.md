qhud v0.8.2 puts the US monthly list price beside each recognized plan. The brighter fill uses $200 as one unit. A $100 plan fills half a unit, a $500 plan fills 2.5 units, and Free shows $0 with an empty fill. The 5H and 7D bars show usage against each window’s limit.

When Codex Business or Claude Team is identified and the seat type is unknown, the chip shows **$20–125** per seat per month. This covers Standard and Premium seats on annual and monthly billing. A verified Codex Standard seat narrows the range to **$20–25**, and a verified Premium seat to **$100–125**. Claude’s **Max 5x** member usage grade can appear alongside the team range. Open the row to check the basis. Prices were reviewed on **2026-11-24**. The invoice gives the actual charge.

Updated the Rust dependency `cookie` to 0.18.3 to address the security advisory. [RUSTSEC-2026-0594](https://rustsec.org/advisories/RUSTSEC-2026-0594)

On 2026-11-24, a local Windows build displayed price chips on individual, team and Free account rows. A separate browser check using synthetic accounts at 361px and 394px window widths measured 64px for $200 and 160px for $500 at both widths.

On Windows, quit qhud from the tray, replace the executable with the copy from the release ZIP and relaunch under the same Windows account to keep using your existing account profiles.
