Each price chip now shows one monthly list price on a blue bar. The `m` suffix means monthly billing. The `y` suffix means annual billing with the price expressed per month. When the cycle is unknown, the chip shows the published monthly rate without a suffix. Free shows **$0**. A full bar represents **$200**, so **$500** spans 2.5 bars.

For Codex Business and Claude Team, a Standard seat shows **$25 m** or **$20 y**. Premium shows **$125 m** or **$100 y**. Claude Team now uses the regular seat type recorded by Claude Code for that account and organization. **Max 5x** remains the member’s usage grade. An unspecified seat shows a starting price such as **$25+**. An identified special Claude seat without a published rate shows **Price unknown**. Expand the row for pricing details.

The Korean UI shows Google AI Pro as **₩29,000 m** for monthly billing or **₩24,167 y** for annual billing at ₩290,000 per year. The fill uses the US monthly equivalent for the same billing cycle, so the width remains comparable across currencies.

Billing-cycle records in `accounts.json` include a verification time and apply only to the matching provider, email, workspace and plan. A paid plan that the provider offers exclusively with monthly billing can show `m` from its published terms.

On Windows, quit qhud from the tray, replace the executable with the copy from the release ZIP and relaunch under the same Windows account. Existing account profiles remain available.
