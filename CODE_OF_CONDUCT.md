# Community conduct

qhud welcomes people who contribute code, documentation, testing, questions,
and careful criticism. We want project spaces to be useful and safe across
languages, experience levels, identities, and abilities.

## How we work together

- Discuss the work, its evidence, and its effect. Challenge ideas without
  attacking the person who raised them.
- Be clear and patient when language or technical background differs. Correct
  mistakes without humiliation and accept good-faith correction.
- Keep feedback relevant and make room for others to participate.
- Protect private information. Redact logs and screenshots, and never expose a
  credential or another person's identity or account data.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, stalking, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, credential disclosure, sustained disruption, and spam are
not accepted.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, releases, and
other spaces where someone represents the qhud project.

For a routine problem, ask for a correction in the same thread without
repeating harmful or private material. For a private or urgent report, use
GitHub's blocking and abuse-reporting tools. Do not use a public issue to
disclose sensitive personal information.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, give a warning, limit participation, or
ban an account according to the severity and pattern of behavior. Reports will
be shared only with people needed to review them. A maintainer involved in a
report should step aside from deciding its outcome when another reviewer is
available.
